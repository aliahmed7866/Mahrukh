BEFORE YOU ASK

The little details.

How do I choose my size?

Open the measurements on the product page and compare with a similar garment you enjoy wearing. Our fabric & fit guide shows how. If a dimension is missing, ask us before ordering. Custom Unstitched means fabric, not a made-to-measure garment.

Where can I check my order?

Your order summary is available under My orders on this device in the browser you used to order. Keep your order reference to request an update on WhatsApp.

What are the delivery and return arrangements?

Read our delivery policy and returns & refunds policy before ordering.
