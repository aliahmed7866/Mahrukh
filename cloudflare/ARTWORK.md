# Mahrukh artwork

The November 2026 lookbook portraits use the user-provided fashion references for clean outlines, relaxed poses, expressive faces and bold textile prints. They depict original Pakistani outfit concepts, not available products. The reference JPGs themselves are not published or committed.

The portraits were generated with the built-in image-generation tool. These are high-detail raster illustrations, delivered as WebP; they are not described as editable SVG. The earlier native SVG studies and their generator remain available in the studio.

Existing document IDs remain stable, and deployment does not rewrite drafts or published content.
