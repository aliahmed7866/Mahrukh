SELLER STUDIO · READ-ONLY FEATURE TOUR

Your shop.
Your way.

A calm little space to manage the details that make Mahrukh yours. These are features of the Flask app; this page is a public design tour.

01 / CONNECT

A friendly hello.

Set your WhatsApp number, email, response hours and Instagram, Facebook, TikTok or YouTube links.

02 / CURATE

Your collection.

Upload real photos from your phone, set sizes and prices, and manage available stock.

03 / FULFIL

Every little order.

Review private order records, confirm requests, track delivery and record verified payments.

04 / DELIVER

Clear expectations.

Choose supported cities, delivery fees and a free-delivery threshold. Publish your own policies.

05 / GET PAID

Start simply.

Configure cash on delivery, merchant transfer instructions or eligible hosted provider invoices, with manual payment verification.

06 / TAKE YOUR TIME

Open when ready.

Keep ordering paused while you prepare. Shop details and policies must be completed before accepting orders.

No seller settings or customer information are stored here. The working seller studio is password-protected in the separately hosted Flask app.
