The little details.

Finding your fit

The live shop shows available sizes and stock. Ask the seller for measurements for your chosen piece; cuts vary. Custom Unstitched means fabric to tailor, not a stitched custom-size service.

Delivery, returns & refunds

The seller publishes her actual service cities, delivery charges, dispatch estimate and return/refund arrangements. Customers review these before ordering. No shipping or return promise is made by this fictional preview.

Your privacy

This static demo does not collect names, phone numbers or addresses. Its sample bag and saved favourites stay in this browser tab’s session storage. You can clear saved pieces from the heart menu. GitHub Pages hosts this demonstration and may process technical access information under its own policies. The live shop has a separate privacy notice and essential session cookie.
