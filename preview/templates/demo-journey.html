ORDER PROGRESS IN THE LIVE SHOP

Clear steps. Human support.

Pending
A request is saved and awaits the seller’s review.
Confirmed
The seller has confirmed the request.
Dispatched
The seller has marked the parcel as sent and can add tracking details.
Completed
The seller has marked the order complete.
Cancelled
Cancellation before dispatch restores reserved stock once.

Payment is checked by the seller.

The app supports configured cash on delivery, manual merchant transfers and eligible hosted invoice links. It does not automatically collect or verify payment. Recording a refund does not send money.

Your information stays private.

The live shop protects order summaries with the customer’s session. On a shared device, customers can remove their access through My orders. That does not delete or cancel the seller’s record.

There is no checkout on GitHub Pages. Do not enter or share real names, addresses, phone numbers or payment details in this preview.
